About this prototype

Nothing on this page is a photograph of a manufactured piece — that piece does not exist yet. The hero and the first two gallery views are the owner's supplied concept renderings. The 3D object is a visual prototype modelled in code from those references so that proportions, materials and presentation can be approved; it is not an engineering or CAD model. Both sizes use one identical geometry. Dimensions, gold weight and gemstones are preliminary targets, not certified specifications.

Darkness is a material.

Black is never empty: it is warm obsidian with grain and depth, so the gold has something to emerge from.

Gold is light, not paint.

Gold appears as reflection, hairline and emblem. Buttons are ivory hairlines; a gold-filled button would cheapen everything around it.

One object per screen.

No grids of products, no badges, no countdowns. The creation always has the room to itself.

Every claim is evidenced.

No invented provenance, clients, valuations or scarcity. Restraint is what makes the remaining statements credible.

Phase 9 · Private acquisition

An introduction,
not a checkout.

There is no cart. The visitor requests access; a private concierge responds personally. Payment happens later, by verified institutional transfer under a signed acquisition agreement.

01Confidential enquiry — encrypted at rest, never shown publicly.
02Personal response from the concierge; identity verification.

05Private client portal: the creation's journal, up to an estimated 80 days.
For owner approval

Before we build further.

These decisions change the product itself, so they are the owner's to make. Nothing below has been assumed in the final implementation.

Product · master reference

A true photograph or CAD file

Both reference boards appear to be composite renders. The prototype follows the first board, top-left image. A real photograph, CAD/STEP file or physical sample is needed to make the model exact.

Product · Grand Collector size

114 × 120 mm, or "two 1.5-litre bottles"?

The board shows 114 × 120 mm (≈ 2.5× the pendant). Two 1.5 L bottles side by side is closer to 180 × 300 mm (≈ 6×). The viewer currently uses the board's figure.

Product · geometry

Arches, doors and finial

The prototype uses five arches per face, a door on four faces, a plain gold finial on the dome, and a diamond bail on the pendant only. Please confirm or correct each.

Brand · name

ILIYA, subject to clearance

Recommended name, pending a trademark search. Also decide how explicitly the building is named in copy; the current draft speaks of Jerusalem's architecture without naming a site.
